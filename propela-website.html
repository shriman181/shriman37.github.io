The founder

Founder & CEO, Propela

"Indian food businesses have extraordinary food. What they lack is extraordinary data. Propela exists to give every operator the same margin intelligence that the largest chains keep to themselves."

Utkarsh Varman founded Propela after seeing firsthand how the information asymmetry between suppliers, operators, and the market was silently bleeding restaurants dry. With a background spanning F&B operations and enterprise software, he built Propela to be the platform he wished had existed: one that speaks the language of a kitchen team, but thinks in the language of a CFO.

Propela's design philosophy — deep workflow integration over surface-level dashboards — comes directly from Utkarsh's conviction that software that lives in daily operations compounds in value in ways that standalone analytics tools never can.
